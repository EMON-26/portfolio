Research

Undergraduate research focused on Banglish mental health classification, model interpretability, and low-resource NLP.

2025 - Present

Bachelor's Thesis: Banglish Mental Health Classification using Transformer-Based Multi-Task Learning

Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Green University of Bangladesh

  • Conducted undergraduate research on automatic mental health classification from Bangla-English code-mixed social media text.
  • Constructed and annotated a large-scale Banglish dataset containing more than 56,000 samples across six mental health categories and three severity levels.
  • Developed a Transformer-based multi-task learning framework using BanglaBERT with Attention Pooling for simultaneous mental health classification and severity prediction.
  • Applied Explainable AI using Grad x Input to improve interpretability and support transparent clinical decision making.
  • Achieved 91.4% classification accuracy with Macro-F1 0.918, and 92.2% severity accuracy with Macro-F1 0.926.
